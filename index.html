Before UVM I was a postdoc of Ehud Hrushovski, and a student of Alexandru Buium. My work tends to revolve around Diophantine equations, differential algebraic geometry, deformation theory, Witt vectors and the Frobenius, absolute geometry, applied model theory, abelian varieties, and AI. I'm happy to talk to anyone about any kind of math; send me an email.

Riemann Hypothesis Resources

Our notes

  • Notes on Alpöge's Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (what I could extract from the Lean so far; please email me with comments, corrections, and suggestions and I will fold them in).
  • The Approach to the Riemann Hypothesis via Hilbert–Pólya Operators and Deninger Cohomology Theories (an overview of theory everyone thought would lead to a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis but didn't; this comes with some incomplete Lean).

Other resources

  • Alpöge's Statement on the proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.
  • Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis — joint statement by the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative.
  • Drew Sutherland's verification page.
  • Elliot Arledge, independent verification receipts — an independent rebuild, axiom audit and nanoda check, on GitHub.
  • Bartosz Naskręcki, riemann-rev (site) — a status dashboard recording who has checked what, with a guide to the route of the proof.
  • A full replay of all 175,187 modules through Lean's kernel, listed on the riemann-rev dashboard as Simon DeDeo's.
  • Alpöge's Lean archive (14 parts, lean.tar.gz.part01–part14).
  • Robin Zhang, A preliminary note on Alpöge's proposed proof of the generalized Riemann hypothesis (Hexagon).
  • Michael Rubinstein, Alpöge/Claude proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis — an AI-generated explanation of the proof for the Riemann zeta function, with a Lean check.
  • Brian Kramer, Unpacking Alpöge's Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (Zenodo preprint) — an exposition written primarily by ChatGPT under Kramer's direction, reverse-engineered from the Lean proof.
  • Distilling the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis: short guide (GitHub, JoseBrox/GRH_distillation) — an AI-assisted effort to distill Alpöge's Lean proof into a shorter human-readable proof; by the repository's own account the human-readable proof is not yet complete.
