Content

This course covers Chapters 1–5 of Dummit and Foote (the basic theory of groups, subgroups, and quotient groups) together with portions of Chapter 6 (finite groups in greater depth, including solvable and nilpotent groups). It is the basis for the continued study of groups, rings, and fields in the sequel, MATH 3552.

Grading

Your grade is determined entirely by in-class exams. There are three midterm exams and a cumulative final exam.

  • Midterm 1 — 20%
  • Midterm 2 — 20%
  • Midterm 3 — 20%
  • Final exam (cumulative) — 40%

There is no graded homework this semester. Problem sets will be posted for practice and worked in class, but they are not collected and do not count toward your grade. Everything that counts is written in class, by hand, without notes or devices. Do the practice problems anyway — they are the exams.

The replacement rule, and why there are no makeups

All exams are closed book: no notes, no books, no calculators, no phones, no devices of any kind.

Each midterm score is replaced by the maximum of that midterm score and your score on the final exam. So a bad day on a midterm is repairable: if you learn the material by December, the final speaks for that midterm too.

One consequence is worth stating plainly. A missed midterm is recorded as a zero, and the replacement rule then replaces that zero with your final exam score. This means there are no makeup exams, and you do not need to document anything or ask my permission to miss one. If you are sick, stay home. The rule already covers you.
